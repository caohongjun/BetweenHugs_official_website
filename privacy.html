1. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

Account and profile. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the information the provider makes available, such as an account identifier, your name, email address, and profile image, depending on the provider and your settings. Apple may provide a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email. You may also provide a display name, profile photo, relationship start date, and other profile details you choose to add.

Shared space. Information in a shared space may include pairing and invitation details; relationship and reunion dates; a reunion place and note you type; the device time zone used to calculate a reunion countdown; photos, captions, and memories; messages, photo attachments, and voice messages; and other content you choose to share. We do not collect your device’s GPS location. A reunion place is text you enter.

Photos. Before a photo is stored, the app re-encodes it on your device and does not keep the original camera metadata. That includes the date, time, and location stored in the photo file. The original remains in your photo library. Please share only content you have the right to share. Shared content can reveal information about you or another person.

Subscription. If you buy Together Plus, Apple or Google processes the payment. We may receive subscription status, product and transaction identifiers, dates, renewal status, and other information needed to provide the subscription. We do not receive your full payment card number from the app stores.

Support. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything else you choose to send.

Service operation. We use Google Firebase to sign you in, store the shared space, and protect the Service. The current version does not use an advertising SDK or a product-analytics SDK. This website does not use analytics tools or advertising trackers.

2. Device permissions

Depending on the features you use, BetweenHugs may ask permission to:

  • access photos or media, so you can choose photos and save them back to your library;
  • use the microphone, so you can record a voice message;
  • send notifications, so we can deliver service-related notices when that feature is available.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Turning a permission off may stop the related feature. BetweenHugs does not ask for camera access or for access to your device’s live location.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; pair you with your partner; provide countdowns, memories, messaging, photo storage, and other features you use; store and display shared content to you and your partner; verify purchases and apply storage limits; respond to support requests and send important service information; keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and fix errors; and comply with law. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

4. How information is shared

Your partner. Content you add to the shared space is meant to be visible to your paired partner. Your partner may view it and save a copy outside the app. Deleting a photo or memory chapter removes it from the shared space. It does not remove copies already saved outside the app.

Service providers. We use Google Firebase to run the Service. We use Apple and Google for sign-in, app distribution, and subscriptions. Those companies handle that information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Other disclosures. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the Service, investigate abuse, or in connection with a transfer of the Service, subject to applicable law.

5. Where information is processed

BetweenHugs is operated from Beijing, China. Account data and shared content are stored with Google Firebase. Information may be processed in China and in the United States, where Firebase is used. The Service is not offered in the United Kingdom or Europe.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information while needed to provide the Service, comply with law, and maintain security.

You can delete photos and memory chapters in the app. After you delete them, your partner can no longer view them in the shared space. Copies already saved outside the app may remain.

If you unpair and the space has photos or memory chapters, the space stays read-only for the period shown in the app so you can save photos, and those photos and memory chapters are then removed. If the space has none, unpairing ends it immediately. A new pairing does not bring back content that has already been removed. The subscription stays with the person who paid.

You can delete your account in the app by opening Settings and then Delete Account. This website does not provide account deletion. Deleting your account removes your sign-in and the content stored with the account. Your partner does not receive your subscription. Copies already saved outside the app may remain.

Copies can remain in routine backups until those backups are replaced. Deleting your BetweenHugs account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel that subscription in the store where you bought it.
